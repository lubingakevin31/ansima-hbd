01 · How it works

A birthday with
a few rules.

  1. Answer a question. One at a time, in order.
  2. Choose. Open a surprise, or move on to the next question.
  3. It is random. A video or a voice message. You will not know which until it opens.
  4. Reach the end. Every video and every voice message unlocks.

Nothing opens before you have played. The story unlocks after your first right answer.
